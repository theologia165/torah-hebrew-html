## Method note
The Notion commentary distinguishes (a) Hebrew syntax/lexicon, (b) rabbinic and medieval reception, (c) patristic reception, and (d) modern literary/compositional observations. Midrashic readings are labeled as such and are not presented as the lexical meaning of the Hebrew text. Where a patristic verse-by-verse comment could not be securely isolated, the commentary reports the broader Genesis 32 reception rather than claiming a direct gloss on that exact verse.
